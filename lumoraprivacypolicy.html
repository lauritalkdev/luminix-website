Privacy Policy for Lumora Wallet

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Lumora Wallet ("Lumora", "we", "our", or "the App") is a self-custody cryptocurrency wallet developed by Luminix.

We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how Lumora handles information when you create, import and use a wallet.

By using Lumora Wallet, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Self-Custody Wallet

Lumora is designed as a self-custody cryptocurrency wallet.

You remain responsible for and in control of your wallet and digital assets. Lumora does not operate as a custodial service and does not take custody of users' cryptocurrency.

Your wallet recovery phrase and/or private keys are used to provide access to your blockchain wallet.

Users are responsible for securely backing up their recovery information. Loss of the information required to recover a wallet may result in permanent loss of access to the wallet and its associated digital assets.

2. Wallet Credentials and Recovery Information

Lumora may generate or process sensitive wallet information including:

  • Wallet recovery phrases
  • Private keys
  • Wallet addresses
  • Wallet PINs and related authentication information

Recovery phrases and private keys are intended to remain under the user's control.

Lumora does not intentionally transmit users' recovery phrases or private keys to Luminix servers for storage.

Sensitive wallet credentials stored by the application on the user's device are protected using the security mechanisms implemented by Lumora.

Users should never disclose their recovery phrase, private key or PIN to another person.

Luminix will never ask a user to provide their recovery phrase or private key through email, social media, customer support or any other communication channel.

3. Information Lumora May Process

To provide wallet functionality, Lumora may process information such as:

  • Public blockchain wallet addresses
  • Cryptocurrency balances
  • Blockchain transaction information
  • Selected blockchain networks and assets
  • Transaction hashes
  • Token information
  • Network and blockchain information
  • Information necessary to obtain cryptocurrency prices and related market information
  • Technical information necessary for application functionality and security

Blockchain addresses and transactions conducted through public blockchain networks may be publicly visible on their respective blockchains.

4. Blockchain Transactions

When you initiate a blockchain transaction, information necessary to execute that transaction may be transmitted to the relevant blockchain network and associated infrastructure providers.

This may include:

  • Public wallet addresses
  • Recipient addresses
  • Transaction amounts
  • Transaction data
  • Transaction hashes
  • Network-related information

Blockchain transactions are generally public and may be permanently recorded on decentralized blockchain networks.

Because these networks are not controlled by Luminix, we cannot modify, reverse or delete information that has already been recorded on a blockchain.

5. Sending and Receiving Digital Assets

Lumora allows users to send and receive supported cryptocurrency and digital assets.

When using these features, blockchain-related information necessary to perform the requested transaction may be communicated to the appropriate blockchain network or infrastructure provider.

Users are responsible for verifying the recipient address, selected network and transaction details before approving a transaction.

6. Cryptocurrency Swaps

Lumora may allow users to exchange supported digital assets through third-party liquidity, swap or blockchain infrastructure providers.

When a user requests a swap, information necessary to obtain quotes and execute the requested transaction may be transmitted to the applicable provider.

Lumora does not transmit your recovery phrase or private key to a swap provider.

Transactions are authorized using the wallet credentials controlled through the user's device.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and terms, which users should review when applicable.

7. Cryptocurrency Prices and Blockchain Data

Lumora may obtain cryptocurrency prices, token information, blockchain balances, transaction histories and other blockchain-related information from third-party APIs, blockchain nodes, RPC services or other infrastructure providers.

When the App communicates with these services, those providers may receive technical network information such as an IP address as part of normal internet communication.

Their handling of such information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Information We Do Not Require for Wallet Creation

Lumora is designed so that creating a self-custody wallet does not require a traditional Lumora account.

Unless a particular feature explicitly states otherwise, Lumora does not require users to provide:

  • A username
  • A traditional account password
  • A phone number
  • Government identification

If future versions of Lumora introduce account-based or identity-dependent services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

9. How Information Is Used

Information processed through Lumora may be used to:

  • Create and operate cryptocurrency wallets
  • Display wallet balances
  • Display supported digital assets
  • Retrieve blockchain information
  • Send and receive digital assets
  • Execute user-requested swaps
  • Estimate blockchain transaction fees
  • Display transaction information
  • Retrieve cryptocurrency market information
  • Protect wallet access
  • Maintain application security and reliability
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve Lumora's functionality

We do not use a user's recovery phrase or private key for advertising or marketing.

10. Information Sharing

Lumora does not sell users' recovery phrases, private keys or personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party infrastructure only where necessary to provide a user-requested feature, such as communicating with:

  • Blockchain networks
  • Blockchain nodes and RPC providers
  • Cryptocurrency pricing services
  • Token-data providers
  • Swap or liquidity providers
  • Other technical infrastructure required for wallet functionality

The information transmitted depends upon the particular feature being used.

11. Security

Lumora uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect wallet information and application data.

Sensitive wallet credentials are designed to be protected locally on the user's device rather than stored as custodial credentials by Luminix.

Users also play an essential role in wallet security.

Users should:

  • Keep their recovery phrase private
  • Never share private keys
  • Use a strong wallet PIN
  • Protect access to their device
  • Verify addresses before sending cryptocurrency
  • Download Lumora only from authorized distribution channels

No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by Lumora remains on the user's device as necessary to provide wallet functionality.

Users may remove locally stored wallet information using the functionality provided by the App or by removing application data/uninstalling the App, where applicable.

Before deleting wallet information, users should ensure that they have securely backed up any recovery information necessary to regain access to their blockchain wallet.

Information permanently recorded on public blockchain networks cannot ordinarily be modified or deleted by Luminix.

Third-party blockchain and infrastructure providers may maintain information according to their respective retention policies.

13. Public Blockchain Information

Public blockchain information should not be considered private.

Transactions may reveal information including:

  • Sending addresses
  • Receiving addresses
  • Cryptocurrency amounts
  • Token transfers
  • Smart-contract interactions
  • Transaction timestamps and hashes

Once a transaction has been submitted and confirmed, Luminix generally cannot remove it from the relevant blockchain.

14. Third-Party Services

Lumora depends on external blockchain networks and may use third-party infrastructure to provide certain functionality.

These services operate independently from Luminix and may maintain their own privacy policies and terms.

Lumora is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

15. Children's Privacy

Lumora is not designed or directed specifically toward children.

We do not knowingly design the service to collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling purposes.

Users must satisfy any applicable age and legal requirements governing cryptocurrency services in their jurisdiction.

16. International Use

Blockchain networks and third-party infrastructure used by Lumora may operate internationally.

As a result, technical information required to process a user's requested blockchain operation may be processed in jurisdictions different from the user's location.

17. Financial and Cryptocurrency Risk

Lumora provides software that enables users to interact with supported blockchain networks and digital assets.

Cryptocurrency values can fluctuate substantially. Blockchain transactions may also be irreversible.

Users are responsible for reviewing transaction information and understanding the risks associated with cryptocurrency and blockchain technology.

Nothing in this Privacy Policy constitutes financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Lumora evolves, including when we introduce new functionality, integrate additional services or change our data practices.

When material changes are made, the Last Updated date at the beginning of this policy will be revised.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
